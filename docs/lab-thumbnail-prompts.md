# Lab thumbnail prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The existing
`packages/nextjs/public/ethereum-101-learning-lab.png` was supplied as a reference
for illustration technique only. These revised scenes use different settings,
compositions and colors. The outputs were resized to 1000 × 563 and saved as
WebP at quality 85 with Sharp.

## Wallets

Asset: `packages/nextjs/public/wallets-desk-learning-lab.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Create a new wide 16:9 thumbnail for an Ethereum Wallets learning lab. The attached image is a reference ONLY for delicate hand-drawn outlines, soft painterly texture and approachable illustration quality. The user wants a much less literal image, with a completely different setting and composition. Scene: an intimate close-up, oblique overhead view of a person's hands at a small desk beside an open window. One hand holds a phone showing a very simple abstract app with a few plain shapes, the other rests beside a small closed notebook and a single ordinary key. Sunlight and leaf shadows cross the desk. Cropped arms only; no crowd, no full room. Convey personal control, care and a first independent step, without explaining technology. Palette: sage green, warm cream, muted teal, restrained apricot highlights. Simple, spacious composition, strong readable shapes at thumbnail size, main objects safely inside central 65%. No holograms, no futuristic laboratory, no Ethereum diamonds, no coins, no padlocks, no shields, no floating icons, no diagrams, no text or numbers, no visible secret words, no logos. Full bleed landscape artwork.

## Crowdfunding Contract

Asset: `packages/nextjs/public/crowdfunding-bridge-learning-lab.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Create a new wide 16:9 thumbnail for a crowdfunding smart contract learning lab. The attached image is a reference ONLY for soft hand-drawn illustration, delicate outlines and welcoming human characters. Make the scene much less explicit, with an entirely different composition and setting. Scene: a wide outdoor view of a small community workshop at dusk, three or four young adults each bringing one wooden piece and working together to assemble a simple arched footbridge in a courtyard. The bridge is a modest shared project, almost complete, a visual metaphor for individual contributions making something possible together. Quiet, believable gestures and clear readable silhouettes, a few simple trees and low buildings in the background, ample sky and breathing room. Do not turn it into a process diagram or an engineering illustration. Warm coral, terracotta and peach light contrasted with dusky indigo and restrained lavender shadows. Soft textured editorial illustration with fine drawn edges, not photorealistic, not glossy 3D. The bridge and people should read at small card size; central subjects remain within the central 70% for cropping. No screens, no futuristic room, no holograms, no floating icons, no coins, no Ethereum logos, no arrows, no code, no gift boxes, no progress bars, no text, no watermark. Full bleed landscape artwork.
